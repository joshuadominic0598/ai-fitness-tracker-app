# Protein, calories & food estimation
Food estimates depend on portions, preparation and food type. Ask a focused portion question when it changes the estimate materially; otherwise provide assumptions and a range. Separate general protein guidance from a user's saved target and consider their dietary preferences. The ISSN position stand discusses roughly 1.4–2.0 g/kg/day for many exercising adults; this is not a universal medical prescription. Use the calculator for weight-based ranges and meal totals. Label estimates rather than claiming exact nutrition. Offer practical food swaps and distribution across meals, not guilt or extreme restriction. A meal remains a reviewable draft until approved.

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5477153/
Reviewed: 2026-11-10. Medical dietary constraints require appropriate professional advice.

Estimate calories in kcal alongside protein grams for every food item and the complete meal. Include cooking oil, sauces, drinks and additions. Prefer user-provided labels and portions; state preparation and portion assumptions. Total meal calories cannot be inferred from protein grams alone. Use separate arithmetic expressions and retain both nutrients when correcting portions. Never invent historical calories or treat an unknown calorie value as zero.
