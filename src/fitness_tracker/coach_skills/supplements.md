# Creatine & supplement literacy
NIH ODS summarizes evidence for creatine mainly in repeated high-intensity work; effects and individual responses vary. Supplements complement an adequate diet rather than replace it. Do not infer deficiency, medical need or a safe personal dose from a tracking goal. Verify the user's product, units and intended use before interpreting quantities. Capsules and IU cannot be converted to grams without product-specific information. Creatine has its own gram target and score; other selected supplements have individual frequency or quantity targets. Log only an explicitly described intake as a draft, not a recommended future dose as completed intake.

Source: https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/ExerciseAndAthleticPerformance-HealthProfessional/
Reviewed: 2026-11-10. Check interactions or medical uncertainty with a qualified clinician.
