# Cardio & endurance
WHO adult public-health guidance describes 150–300 minutes of moderate aerobic activity, or 75–150 minutes vigorous activity weekly, with muscle strengthening on at least two days. These ranges are population guidance, not an immediate prescription for an inactive person or someone with symptoms. Start from current capacity, preferred activities and available time. Easy conversational sessions can make a routine approachable. Increase demands gradually and adapt to feedback. Do not invent heart-rate zones or performance forecasts. Use the calculator for duration, pace, unit conversion or combined activity totals.

Source: https://www.who.int/europe/publications/i/item/9789240014886
Reviewed: 2026-11-10. Ask about limitations before individualizing intensity.
