# Strength & progression
Resistance exercise improves strength; heavier loading can favor maximal-strength gains in healthy adults. Progress should reflect experience, technique, recovery and repeated logged performance. Choose repeatable movements, manageable starting loads and small changes; do not infer a safe maximum from an estimated 1RM. Separate tested PRs from estimates. Give a measurable next-session milestone and a review point. Consistency and individual preference matter; a complex program is not automatically superior.

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12965823/
Reviewed: 2026-11-10. Findings in healthy adults are not universal prescriptions.
