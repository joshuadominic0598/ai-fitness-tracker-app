# Full-body & sustainable routines
Cover major muscle groups with a practical mix of knee-dominant, hip-dominant, pushing, pulling and trunk tasks. A full-body layout is an organizational choice, not a proven requirement for everyone. Fit the user's available days and equipment; offer a shorter fallback session for busy weeks. Avoid assigning every exercise in the catalog. Explain the purpose of each movement and leave recovery between demanding sessions. Revisit the plan using completion, comfort and performance.
